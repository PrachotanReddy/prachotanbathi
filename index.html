Work & interests

Architecture

Arm System Architecture

My work at Arm spans the boundaries between hardware, firmware, and software. I help define system security requirements and interfaces, working with standards organizations and open-source projects to connect architectural specifications with implementation.

  • System architecture
  • Standards
  • Open source

Questions & considerations

  • How do you ensure an off-the-shelf OS boots on an Arm machine?
  • Where should security responsibilities and trust boundaries sit between components?
  • How can specifications define behavior precisely enough for interoperable implementations?

Security architecture

Platform Security

I help define security architecture for Arm systems, including Trusted Platform Modules (TPMs), Caliptra, platform Root of Trust (pRoT), Arm Confidential Compute Architecture (CCA), and remote attestation. The architectural questions concern trust boundaries, workload isolation, and the evidence a platform provides about its state.

  • TPM
  • Caliptra
  • xRoT
  • Arm CCA
  • Remote attestation

Questions & considerations

  • What assumptions underpin the root of trust, and which components remain trusted?
  • What does attestation evidence establish, and how should a verifier appraise it?
  • How do isolation and attestation address distinct parts of a system’s threat model?

Prototyping

System Manageability

I prototype manageability architecture for Arm systems, including baseboard management controllers (BMCs), the Security Protocol and Data Model (SPDM), and reliability, availability, and serviceability (RAS). This work connects management interfaces with platform security and fault handling.

  • BMC
  • SPDM
  • RAS architecture
  • OpenBMC

Questions & considerations

  • How should a BMC communicate with an SoC and other system components?
  • How should SPDM authentication and measurements be implemented?
  • How should a system report faults, contain their effects, and support recovery (RAS)?

Research interest

AI Safety & Security

I’m exploring AI safety and the security of agentic systems from a systems architecture perspective. My interests include how agents are granted access to tools and data, how that authority is constrained, and how controls can be evaluated against unintended actions and adversarial inputs.

  • AI safety
  • AI security
  • Agentic systems

Questions & considerations

  • Which actions can an agent take, and what limits its authority over tools and data?
  • How can we evaluate whether controls hold under adversarial inputs and unexpected tool behavior?
  • What can an evaluation demonstrate about safety, and where does uncertainty remain?

Research interest

Hardware-enforced AI Safety

I’m interested in how hardware-enforced isolation and access controls could constrain AI systems, and what attestation could tell us about those controls. This is an exploratory interest grounded in my platform security work. I participated in the Hardware Assurance Programme’s 2026 cohort and welcome discussions with researchers in AI safety and security.

  • Hardware isolation
  • Access control
  • Attestation

Questions & considerations

  • Which constraints can hardware enforce, and what assumptions do those guarantees depend on?
  • What can attestation establish about the enforcement environment and its configuration?
  • Which safety properties still require software controls or behavioral evaluation?
